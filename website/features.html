Features in detail

JTech Browser is made for long sessions with many tabs. Here is everything it can do.

Vertical tabs with superpowers

Tabs run down a resizable sidebar instead of across the top. Pin the ones you always need, gather related ones in groups and drag them wherever you want. Idle tabs can sleep to free memory, and your session comes back after a restart.

  • Drag & drop reordering
  • Tab groups with colors and collapse
  • Sleep mode for inactive tabs
  • Session restore on startup

Split View

Split view shows two tabs side by side or stacked, with a divider you can move. Compare two documents, keep a chat open next to a video or monitor a dashboard while you work.

  • Vertical or horizontal split
  • Movable divider
  • Swap and close panes quickly

Peek View

Shift + click a link to open a temporary Peek window above the current page. Read the content and close it again without ever leaving your current tab.

  • Quick inline previews
  • No clutter in your tab list
  • Close with Esc or a click outside

Built-in ad and tracker blocking

Every request a page makes is checked in Rust with Brave's open-source adblock-rust engine. EasyList and EasyPrivacy are on by default; the uBlock filters, Peter Lowe's list and Fanboy's Annoyance list can be added.

  • Network-level filtering in Rust
  • Automatic list updates
  • Per-site allowlist
  • Cosmetic filter support

Audio Hub and media controls

The Audio Hub lists every tab that plays sound, with play and pause, volume per tab and sites that always start muted. Music in a background tab shows up at the bottom of the sidebar with title and controls.

Design Studio

Change the whole look in one click with a preset, or step by step. Every change applies right away. Duplicate, import and export themes to share them.

  • Theme gallery with dark, light, vivid and calm presets
  • Custom colors and CSS
  • Animated backgrounds, custom cursors and trails
  • Customizable start page with clock and quick links
